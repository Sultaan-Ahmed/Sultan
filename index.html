About

From Sketch to Shipment,
the Sultan Ahmed Story.

I am Sultan Ahmed — currently the sole Bangladesh merchandising lead at AURA (UK) Liaison Office, Dhaka. I independently manage end-to-end execution across 8 reputed UK buyer accounts, from concept development through bulk execution to final shipment, alongside core basic styling programmes.

My specialism runs deep into the technical side of the garment — pattern correction and grading, resolving fit comments through to buyer sign-off, and material consumption analysis in Lectra CAD — paired with hands-on vendor assessment and sourcing experience across factories in Bangladesh, China and Sri Lanka.

With a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Textile Engineering from DUET, Gazipur, and a running PGD in Garments Business from IBA, University of Dhaka, I bring both technical precision and commercial acumen to every programme I manage.
